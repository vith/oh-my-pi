## What

Fix the background bash card regression reported after #12. Running bash cards remain mutable until their terminal result instead of retiring into immutable terminal history when the call backgrounds. Preserve the existing detached-task retirement behavior.

Extend the existing live/rebuilt-card regression with acknowledged terminal-history pressure, remove an obsolete test that required background bash updates to stop, and document compact live cards under viewport pressure.

## Why

The walkthrough card stopped repainting after roughly 30 seconds even though the job kept producing output. Reproduction showed the card retiring when later conversation exceeded the viewport, not a 30-second job timeout.
