## What

Show a numbered UI-handled stream update and its normalized event kind alongside reasoning-update age in ANSI and native terminals. Preserve the number, kind and timestamp through smooth reveal and repaint paths.

The number counts handled snapshots, not tokens or network packets; coalesced events may become one UI update. Empty or identical reasoning events now have a visible explanation for resetting the clock.

## Why

Follow-up to #16: the previous clock could reset when a reasoning event supplied no readable text, leaving the user unable to see what changed. This explains those resets without presenting them as proof of ongoing inference. The specific event behind the user's original observation was not captured.
